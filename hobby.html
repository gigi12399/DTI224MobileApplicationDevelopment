My Hobby: Diabetes-Friendly Baking

My hobby is baking — specifically making desserts that my mother can safely enjoy. After she was diagnosed with diabetes, she could no longer eat the sweets she loved, such as cakes, chocolate chip cookies, eclairs, and chocolate mousse cake, because all of them are made with regular sugar, which raises blood sugar levels.

I did not want her to miss out on the things she loves. So I started learning how to recreate those desserts using diabetes-friendly ingredients — sweeteners that do not spike blood sugar — and I make real-fruit jams from scratch instead of using store-bought ones that contain added sugar. Everything is made entirely by me, from selecting the ingredients to the finished dessert.

When my mother eats the desserts I bake, her blood sugar level stays stable. That result is what motivates me to keep baking and to keep improving my recipes.

What I treasure most is the moment I see her reaction. When I bake for her, I can see the happiness in her eyes — she looks so pleased and content. She eats really well and calls out to me non-stop saying "my daughter, my daughter" all day long. Her absolute favourite is chocolate mousse cake, and she also loves oreo balls. Watching her enjoy every bite is a feeling I cannot describe in words.

Now that I am studying abroad in Bangkok, it has been a long time since I last baked for her. She has not been able to eat those desserts since I left. I miss my mother very much, and more than anything, I look forward to going home and baking for her again.

Desserts I Make:

Chocolate Chip Cookies Classic cookies made with sugar substitutes so they taste just as good without affecting blood sugar.
Eclair Choux pastry filled with cream and topped with chocolate glaze — all made with diabetes-friendly ingredients.
Chocolate Mousse Cake A light, rich cake made with unsweetened chocolate and alternative sweeteners.
Oreo Balls One of my mother's favourites — made with diabetes-friendly ingredients so she can enjoy them without worry.
Real Fruit Jam Homemade jam using only real fruits with no added sugar — used as filling or topping for other desserts.

Why This Hobby Matters to Me:

  • My mother loves desserts but could not eat them after her diabetes diagnosis
  • Commercial shops do not offer truly diabetes-safe versions of these desserts
  • I research and test recipes myself to find the right substitute ingredients
  • I make real-fruit jams by hand to avoid store-bought jams with added sugar
  • When she eats my baking, her blood sugar level does not go up
  • Seeing her enjoy the desserts she loves is the reward that keeps me going
